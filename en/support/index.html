Support

If you have a question or the app does not work as expected, email us. We will do our best to help.

Before contacting us

  • make sure you have the latest version of the app installed;
  • check your device volume if you cannot hear the narration;
  • close and reopen the app;
  • if an issue keeps happening, include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps to reproduce it.

About the app

Mafia without a host helps you play without a separate host: it assigns roles, guides the stages of the game with voice prompts, and helps determine when the game is over.
